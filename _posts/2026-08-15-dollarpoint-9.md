Summer Yoga at Dollar Point consists of Friday yoga on the grass by the pool and Saturday yoga on the beach by the lake. Each weekends class has the same theme and a similar series of poses, but each is uniquely suited to the setting.  

## Friday Poolside Yoga Outline


### Grounding

- Gratitude
- Meditation

### Gentle Warm-Up

- Banana pose
- Reclined twist with strap
- Happy baby
- Seated side bends
- Table top 

###  Stretch and Strengthen

- Childs pose to modified plank flow
- Down dog 
- High to low lunge flow with 'Happy, Healthy, Free' mantra
- Warrior I to humble warrior flow
- Dancing Shiva
- Dancer pose
- Partner dancer pose
- Warrior II to triangle to reverse triangle 
- Star to wide-leg forward fold to side lunge

### Cool down

- Legs up the wall and plow pose
- Bridge 
- Free flow
- Savasana 
- Meditation: 'Happy, Healthy, Free' 

## Beach Outline


### Grounding

- Gratitude
- Meditation

### Gentle Warm-Up

- Side lying pose
- Reclined twist
- Happy baby
- Seated neck circles
- Seated forward folds
- Seated twists
- Table top
- Cobra


###  Stretch and Strengthen

- Childs pose to modified plank flow
- Down dog with figure four legs
- High to low lunge flow with 'Happy, Healthy, Free' mantra
- Warrior I to humble warrior flow
- Dancing Shiva
- Partner dancer pose
- Warrior II to triangle to reverse triangle 
- Star to wide-leg forward fold to side lunge
- Headstand or down dog with head on sand

### Cool down

- Prone shoulder stretch
- Flipped dog
- Pigeon 
- Free flow
- Savasana 
- Meditation: 'Happy, Healthy, Free'
